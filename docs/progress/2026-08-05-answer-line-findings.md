## No answer line (13), and why

**No single sourced claim exists — the page is method or guidance.** The honest
fix is depth work, not a manufactured sentence.

- `/money/budgeting` — budgeting methods; nothing to cite.
- `/money/debt` — payoff strategy; nothing to cite.
- `/money/investing` — education on what instruments are; "how to start" is
  guidance. A possible future answer around TFSA/robo minimums would need a
  source that states them.
- `/money/credit` — explains how scores work. A claim like "payment history
  and utilisation drive your score" is citable in principle (Equifax/TransUnion)
  but is not currently stated crisply enough in the page to stand as its answer.
  Closest to promotable of the 13.
- `/money/seven-generations` — an essay; an answer line would be false apparatus.

**Situational by design** (the brief expected these to end up here):

- `/path/first-job` — walkthrough of a first pay stub. The deduction rates are
  already registry-fed in the body; no single question is being answered.
- `/path/leaving-home`, `/path/building-life`, `/path/career-growth`,
  `/path/giving-back` — life-stage guidance, no factual spine.
- `/self/confidence`, `/self/conversations`, `/self/stress` — wellness pages;
  provenance apparatus does not belong on them.

## For the depth session

The five `/money` pages without strips are the real worklist: each could earn
an answer line by adding the factual, citable core the strip needs (e.g. basic-
account features for budgeting, collection-agency rules for debt, account
minimums for investing, score factors for credit). The `/self` pages should
stay strip-free on purpose — that is a design position, not a gap.
