## The 5 already checked against a source

Skim these. Flag only what is wrong.

- **Section 87 of the Indian Act exempts from taxation the personal property of a Status Indian or a band situated on a reserve.**
  Indian Act, RSC 1985, c I-5, s. 87 — https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/i-5/section-87.html
  - [ ] Wrong or out of date
- **The connecting-factors test for locating income comes from the Supreme Court of Canada's decision in Williams v. Canada.**
  Williams v. Canada, [1992] 1 SCR 877
  - [ ] Wrong or out of date
- **The connecting factors that matter for employment income include where the duties are performed, where the employer is located, who benefits from the work, and where the employee lives.**
  CRA — Indian Act Exemption for Employment Income Guidelines — https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/indigenous-peoples/information-indians.html
  - [ ] Wrong or out of date
- **Exempt employment income is reported in Box 71 of the T4 slip.**
  CRA — T4 slip, Box 71 (Indian (exempt income) – Employment) — https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/completing-filing-information-returns/t4-information-employers/t4-slip.html
  - [ ] Wrong or out of date
- **TFSA growth is already tax-free for everyone, so Section 87 is not needed for it.**
  CRA — Tax-Free Savings Account — https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/tax-free-savings-account.html
  - [ ] Wrong or out of date
